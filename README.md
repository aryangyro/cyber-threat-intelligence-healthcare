# Cyber Threat Intelligence — Healthcare Case Study

A practical project exploring how cyber threat intelligence works in a real organisation, not just in theory.

## What this is
A 1,945-word intelligence report on a medium healthcare provider whose
security team was overwhelmed by alerts while a real attack was taking
shape: phishing emails to clinical staff, harvested credentials, abnormal
VPN logins outside business hours, lateral movement between servers,
PowerShell executions, and large volumes of encrypted files on a shared
drive.

Indicators in the case:
- Malicious IP: 185.220.101.1
- Suspicious domain: secure-login-health.com
- Malware SHA-256: a1b2c3d4e5f67890abcdef1234567890abcdef1234567890abcdef1234567890

## What it covers
- From Data to Intelligence — how raw alerts become information, then
  intelligence, and why that last step keeps failing
- CTI Classification — Technical, Tactical, Operational, Strategic, who
  each one is for and what decisions it supports
- CTI Lifecycle — Planning & Direction, Collection, Processing, Analysis,
  Production, Dissemination, Feedback & Continuous Improvement
- Maturity Assessment — TIMM, Hunt Maturity Model, Detection Maturity Level
- Intelligence-Driven Recommendations — Technology, Processes, People

No threat actor is named, because the evidence in the case does not support
attribution — the report says that explicitly.
